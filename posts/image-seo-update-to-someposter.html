Image SEO update to SomePoster

SomePoster now gives you the option to add keyword overlays in all images you upload.

You click the checkbox "Keyword on image", and SomePoster automatically change your image, and place the first words of your post as readable text on the image.

This is very good for image seo, as search engines, especially Google, read this text, to better understand what the image and post is about.

If you have chosen to use spintax, to get unique posts on all platforms, the image text will be different on each image, according to what the specific platforms post is...

Keyword on image is available at all plans. SomePoster starts at 7USD/month, but I recommend getting the 39USD/month plan..
